FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What is Wildstack?

A design platform and service for franchises and multi-location brands. We build your brand-locked templates. Then your team turns one-line briefs into print, digital and motion assets in minutes, or our team does it within 48 hours.

Can our team run it in‑house?

Yes. With Enterprise, your team and partners brief the platform directly and get finished, on-brand assets in minutes. We build and maintain your templates, you run production.

How much does it cost?

Agency starts from $2,500 a month, sized to how many assets you need. Enterprise is an annual contract based on your number of locations. Book a call and we’ll size it with you.

What can you make?

Print-ready flyers and posters, digital assets for social, ads and screens, and motion and video. All from your own brand templates.

How do we brief you?

Send one line in Slack, email or a short form: what changes, and where it goes.

How fast do we get files?

Within 48 hours of your brief, Monday to Friday. A brief you send on Friday comes back by Tuesday.

What if we need changes?

Revisions are included. Pin a comment on the exact spot and we fix it.

Who sets up our templates?

We do. We build them from your brand kit and lock every rule.

Are the files print-ready?

Yes. CMYK, with bleed and crop marks, ready for your printer.

What counts toward our monthly files?

Each finished file counts once. A new size of the same design is a new file.

Do we still need our agency?

Keep them for campaigns. Send us the repeat work.
