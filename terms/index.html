2. The Agency service

We design print, digital and motion assets from your briefs, using brand templates we build for you. Your plan sets how many assets you get each month: 50, 100, 300 or 600.

  • Turnaround: we deliver within 48 hours of a complete brief, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. A brief is complete once we have all the copy, images and details we need.
  • Revisions: Two rounds of revisions per asset are included. Changes that need a new layout or a new template count as a new asset.
  • Unused assets: Unused assets don’t roll over to the next month.
  • What’s not included: campaign strategy, printing, media buying and photography, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

3. Your part

You’ll send clear briefs, approve or give feedback in reasonable time, and make sure you have the rights to everything you send us, such as logos, photos, fonts and partner names. You’re responsible for checking final files before you print or publish them, including prices, dates, legal text and QR codes. Once you approve a file, it’s final.

4. Payment

  • Plans are billed monthly in advance in USD. Prices on our website exclude taxes, which we add where required.
  • Invoices are due within 14 days. If a payment is more than 14 days late, we may pause work until it’s paid.
  • You can cancel with 2 weeks’ written notice before the end of your current billing period. Your plan then ends at the end of that period. There is no setup fee and no minimum term.
  • We may change our prices with at least 30 days’ notice. Changes apply from your next billing period.

5. Who owns what

  • Your files: once you’ve paid for them, you own the final assets we make for you and the brand templates we design for you.
  • Your materials: your logos, photos and brand stay yours. You give us permission to use them only to deliver our service.

  • Showing our work: we won’t show your name, logo or work in our portfolio or marketing without your written permission.
