01 · Foundation

Logo System

The Wild Stack wordmark across all approved variants and background contexts. Always use an approved variant — never modify the logo.

Primary · Dark Background
Coral Background
Light Background
Deep Background
Wordmark · Variant A
Wordmark · Variant B
✓ Do
  • Use on approved backgrounds only — dark, deep dark, or coral
  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "w" on all sides
  • Use the SVG file for web at any size above 120px wide
  • Use the PNG file for social assets and print under 1000px
  • Keep the wordmark monochromatic when overlaying photography
✗ Don't
  • Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the logo
  • Do not change the font, spacing, or colour of the wordmark
  • Do not place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or effects
  • Do not use the logo below 80px wide in any context
